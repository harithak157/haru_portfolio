ACTIVITY 03

FSA in Tamil

Finite-State Representation and Tamil Morphotactics

Finite-State Automata Tamil Morphology NLP

About the Activity

This activity explores Finite-State Automata (FSA) and Tamil morphotactics through a handwritten FSA diagram and supporting notes. The materials illustrate how a root can combine with grammatical suffixes such as plural, tense and case markers to form different word forms.

It connects Tamil word formation with the idea of representing valid morphological combinations through states and transitions.

Objectives

  • Review the uploaded finite-state automaton diagram.
  • Understand the role of roots and suffixes in Tamil word formation.
  • Identify examples of plural, tense and case markers in the notes.
  • Observe how morphological information can be represented as sequences of states or transitions.
