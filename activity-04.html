PROJECT OVERVIEW

Broadway Map

A location-based, show-aware Broadway theatre recommendation system that identifies and ranks nearby theatres and shows based on genre/mood preferences, verified show details, and travel accessibility.

NER Information Extraction Semantic Similarity Text Classification

Research Gaps

01

Show-specific theatre matching

Identifies theatres currently showing productions relevant to the user’s preferred genre, mood, language, or show type.

02

Show and availability verification

Considers verified show details, schedules, ticket availability, accessibility features, and venue amenities.

03

Travel accessibility

Ranks suitable theatres based on walking, subway, or road distance and estimated travel time.

Role of NLP

01

Theatre and show information extraction

Extract show titles, genres, cast, schedules, venue features, and accessibility details from listings, descriptions, and reviews.

02

Understanding natural-language queries

Identify show names, genres, actors, mood, location, and user search intent from natural-language queries.

03

Semantic matching

Match different terms describing equivalent genres, moods, or show types using semantic similarity.

NLP-Specific Skills

01

Named Entity Recognition (NER)

Identifying important entities such as show titles, theatre names, actors, genres, and locations.

02

Information Extraction

Extracting show schedules, ticket availability, venue amenities, and accessibility information from text.

03

Semantic Similarity

Finding similarities between different expressions with equivalent meanings, such as “family-friendly” and “kid-friendly.”

04

Text Classification

Classifying show descriptions and reviews by genre, suitability, sentiment, or accessibility.

Learning Outcomes

  • Understood how NLP can support location-based Broadway show and theatre recommendations.
  • Identified research gaps in show-specific theatre matching and accessibility-aware ranking.
  • Explored the role of NER and information extraction in theatre and show listings.
  • Understood how semantic similarity can match equivalent entertainment and genre terms.
  • Identified NLP techniques relevant to the proposed system.
